Authenticating a virutil transfer on a Windows host

Not shipping code. A model of one push so the three candidate answers in docs/contract.md §7 can be driven by hand and watched where they hurt.

The question. On a Linux host the share is anonymous (map to guest = Bad User) and the guest fetches with no credential. Windows has no equivalent that is on by default. Three ways out were written down and none picked. This drives the whole lifecycle of a transfer — stage, credential, publish, fetch, tear down — under each, and lets you end it badly (Ctrl-C, host reboot, a second transfer) to see what each one leaves behind on the host.

Watch for: what survives an abort, what survives a reboot, and which option needs Administrator for which step.
